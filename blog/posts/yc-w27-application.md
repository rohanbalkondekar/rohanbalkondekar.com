### What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

The goal for VibeGrow now is to go beyond software factories and marketing factories and build business factories. Meaning just make businesses work all via Agent Swarms.

We want to remove the expert bottleneck across businesses, with agent swarms handling work that previously required specialist knowledge, so starting and running a business no longer depends on assembling a team of experts.

We are not inventing a new business model. The idea is to take what already works, could be a traditional business, and make it work from scratch with zero human intervention, handled entirely by agent swarm driven growth experiments - Starting with a fully autonomous perfume influencer.

We now clearly see that multiple agents working together are fully capable to run a business that would generate $1M in revenue in next 6 months.
