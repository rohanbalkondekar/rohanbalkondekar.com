The idea behind it

A “no” is an outcome.
Not asking is a choice.

I want to find out how long it takes to hear 1,000 real rejections. The first commitment is 1,000 distinct asks in six months—not 1,000 guaranteed rejections.

Success is taking a meaningful action. An acceptance is a win. A rejection is information. Silence stays pending; it never becomes a “no” just to move a counter.

Only confirmed submissions or completed asks count toward the goal. Preparation stays in progress. The challenge starts on 22 October 2026; an entry’s submission date is recorded separately, only when known.
